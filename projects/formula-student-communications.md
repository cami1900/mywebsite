# E-Agle TRT: Formula Student Communications

**Tools:** Figma, PowerPoint · **Where:** E-Agle TRT, University of Trento Formula Student team · **When:** 10.2022 – 08.2024 (Chief Technician of Communications from Sept 2023)

## What it is
E-Agle TRT designs and builds electric race cars for Formula Student competitions. I started as a graphic designer and went on to lead the communications department.

## Highlights
- **Leadership:** coordinated a multidisciplinary team of **12 people** across photography, video production and event management, managing activities and deadlines.
- **Sponsor relations:** managed relationships with sponsors and the team's technical communications.
- **Engineering storytelling:** created social media content and presentations about the car's engineering for sponsors, trade fairs and Formula Student competitions.
