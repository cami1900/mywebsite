# BOM Automation & Project Planning Tool

**Tech:** Excel (advanced formulas), PowerPoint · **Where:** Pedrollo S.p.A., San Bonifacio (VR), Italy, R&D Training Program internship · **When:** 05.2026 – 08.2026

## What it is
A set of tools built during my R&D internship at a pump manufacturer to make engineering work faster and easier to track.

## Highlights
- **Automated BOM generation:** an Excel model that works out component types and quantities directly from pump model codes and configurations, so bills of materials no longer had to be compiled by hand.
- **Project planning and forecasting tool:** an Excel tracker for project timelines that improved tracking and communication between the **R&D** and **Industrialization** teams.
- **Market research:** competitor benchmarking and PowerPoint presentations to support product development and portfolio decisions.
